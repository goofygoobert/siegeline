# Plan: the next three regions

Each region reuses the engine, saves, UI and tools unchanged. It needs data files (units, enemies, stages), Blender scripts for the new cast and scenery, one music variation, and a balance pass. A region is roughly one night of the same pipeline used for region 1.

## Region 2: The Bell Marshes (9 stages: 6 + boss + 1 side + 1 challenge)
- **Setting:** drowned chapels in a fog marsh whose bells still ring at night. Cold teal fog over the ember palette.
- **New units:** **Pikewall** (anti-large, double damage to brutes), **Sapper** (runs to the gate and blows a big hole in it, only hits buildings), **Kestrel Rider** (first flyer, ignores the ground line).
- **New enemies:** Wailer (flying shade, forces flyers and archers into play), Bog Husk (slows on hit), plus marsh variants of the region 1 Hollows.
- **New mechanic:** fog banks drift across the field and hide units inside them. Flares and the Bellringer's aura reveal them.
- **Boss: the Mire Choir.** Three linked bell-wraiths. Hit one and the others heal it, so you must split damage. Warnings: a rising chime before a sonic cone, and bells that glow before a heal pulse.
- **Side stage:** Night Watch (no hero, permanent fog). **Challenge:** Endless Tide, survive wave after wave with a star track for score.

## Region 3: The Gilded Causeway (10 stages)
- **Setting:** a white-and-gold toll road of the mercenary Tithe, with banners, carts and coin chests.
- **New units:** **Flame Monk** (cone fire against swarms), **Chain Warden** (pulls one enemy forward out of formation), **Crossbow Cart** (long-range siege that pierces lines).
- **New enemies:** Tithe Spearman (armoured), Tithe Crossbow (outranges your archers), Gilded Paladin (heals other Tithe, armoured). These are the first enemies that punish spamming.
- **New mechanic:** armour. A shield icon marks reduced damage, so Pikewalls and Sappers matter.
- **Boss: Abbot-Banker Corvane.** He buys mercenaries mid-fight with coin (you can see the coin stack he spends from) and throws a gold shield on allies. Breaking his coin cart stops the hiring.

## Region 4: Hollowdeep Mines (10 stages)
- **Setting:** lantern-lit mine shafts, rails and minecarts, molten seams. Darker stages lit mostly by your own lanterns.
- **New units:** **Grave Priest** (revives one fallen unit), **Banner Knight** (aura +20% damage), **Ironclad** (big armoured tank).
- **New enemies:** Maw Hound (fast pack beast), Slag Golem (molten tank that leaves fire), Cart Bomber (rides a minecart down the rail, so you must shoot it with the ballista before it hits).
- **New mechanic:** the rail. Minecarts roll along the field on a schedule and hit both sides, so timing your sends matters.
- **Boss: the Slag Mother.** She rises from a lava pool, splitting the lane with molten rivers that block walking for a few seconds (shown on the ground first).

## Steady unlock pace (whole game)
3 new units per region in regions 2–5, 2 in region 6 (20 in total). Each first clear unlocks something: a unit, a power upgrade tier, or a new Armoury row (castle walls tier 2, a second ballista bolt type in region 3).
