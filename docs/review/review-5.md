# Review 5: fresh reviewer, new battle frame and menu against well-made phone strategy games (2026-11-17)

The reviewer compared on craft only (their references: three well-known lane-defence and tower-defence phone games) and saw the frame before the fixes below.
Verdict on the battle frame: **no**, not yet professional: "the painted sunset backdrop is at professional level; everything in front of it is not." Menu: "nearly, as a clean app; no, as a game." Characters: "halfway."
